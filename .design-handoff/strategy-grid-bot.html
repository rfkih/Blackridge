Grid Bot.

A market-neutral algorithm that sets up an evenly-spaced ladder of buy and sell orders inside a price range. Buys when price drops to the next rung, sells when it rises. Profits from volatility — no directional bias required.

How the algorithm works

1
Define the range

You set an upper and lower bound based on recent support/resistance. Default: 30-day Bollinger Bands at 2σ.

3
Trigger on touch

As price hits a buy rung, the algo fills the order and immediately places a sell order one rung above. Spread = profit.

2
Slice into N grids

The range is divided into 5–50 evenly-spaced rungs (you choose). More grids = more trades but smaller per-trade profits.

4
Auto-rebalance

If price breaks the range, the algo can either pause, rebalance to a new range, or exit — you choose the policy on deploy.

Frequently asked

Things people ask before signing up

Where does my capital live?

On your own exchange account. We never custody funds — we only get read-only API keys plus trading permissions (without withdrawal). Your money stays where you put it; we just send orders on your behalf.

How do you calculate performance fees?

High-water mark, monthly. We only charge on net new profits above your previous all-time high — so we don't double-charge if a bot has a bad month and recovers.

What happens if a strategy starts losing?

Every algo has a hard drawdown limit you set on deploy (default: -15%). If the bot hits that line it pauses automatically and notifies you. You decide whether to resume, retune, or close.

Can I bring my own strategy?

Yes — Pro and Institution include our visual strategy builder. You can also bring Python/JS via our API. Institution clients work directly with our quant team to engineer custom alpha.

Is this legal in my country?

Machiavelli is a tool — the legality of crypto trading is determined by your local laws and your exchange's terms. We don't operate in OFAC-sanctioned countries or US restricted states. Check your jurisdiction before signing up.
